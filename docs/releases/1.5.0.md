# CarSync Music 1.5.0 — by Brayo

Proposed review release only. Creator and maintainer: Brayo · +254 740 636 336 · https://github.com/moses-code-master.

Adds a Videos library, MP4/MKV/WebM imports subject to device codecs, thumbnails, local video playlists, single-player inline/fullscreen playback and optional audio-only video. Extends authenticated clock/timeline synchronization to matching local videos, including shared controls, next-video progression and reconnect. Missing/mismatched files are reported explicitly. Video is local-only; no video relay, paid hosting or upload-limit increases.

Pictures require parked confirmation; Driving Mode blocks pictures and backgrounding revokes confirmation. Optional Automotive UX-restriction integration fails closed. Generic units do not provide reliable motion detection. Existing music, creator branding, app ID/signing and saved data are preserved through a Room migration.

See ../VIDEO-GUIDE.md and the pull request for test results, physical validation limits, review APK and two-screen instructions. Official release publication requires approval.
